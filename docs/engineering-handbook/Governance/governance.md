# Governance and roles 

This page summarises the governance functions and decision rights that keep the interoperability framework stable and enforceable.

## Governance functions

**Interoperability Governance Board (IGB)**  
Owns the interoperability framework and approves authoritative versions of:
- level definitions,
- requirements catalogues (REQ/SEC/NODE),
- normative process changes (including validation impacts).

**Technical Validation Team (TVT)**  
Executes conformance testing and produces conformance reports. Maintains validation tooling and test suites.

**Operational Federation Team (OFT)**  
Coordinates federation operations and ensures monitoring and revalidation triggers are applied consistently across nodes.

**Change Control Secretariat (CCS)**  
Administers change requests, decision records, version publication, and provider/operator communications.

## Decision rights (RACI)

| Area | Accountable | Admin | Consulted | Informed |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Requirements catalogues (REQ/SEC/NODE) | IGB | CCS | TVT, OFT | Providers/Operators |
| Validation rules/tooling | TVT | CCS | OFT | IGB, Providers/Operators |
| Node/federation operational requirements | OFT | CCS | TVT | IGB, Providers/Operators |
| Templates and onboarding artefacts | CCS | CCS | TVT, OFT | Providers/Operators |

## Charter and review cadence

Decision rights MUST be documented in a governance charter and reviewed at least annually. Changes to decision rights MUST be recorded as governance decisions.
